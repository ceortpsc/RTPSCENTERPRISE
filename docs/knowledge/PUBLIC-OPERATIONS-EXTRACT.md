# RTPSC Enterprise — Public Operations Extract

This file is a public-safe derivative of the internal RTPSC operations directive. It intentionally excludes internal prompts, private staff data, tax credentials, vault references, private endpoints, taxpayer examples and non-public pricing exceptions.

## Public service divisions

- Ross Tax Pro Software Co. — corporate administration and shared services.
- Tax Practitioner Virtual Office / 254-Tax Consultants — tax preparation, amendments, consultations and taxpayer-support workflows.
- Ross Tax Pro Software — tax workflow software and technical support.
- Ross Prime Payroll — payroll, timekeeping and employer workflows.
- Ross Tax Pro University / Ross Tax Academy — tax education and learner support.
- Ross E-Drive University — driver-education enrollment and learning workflows.
- Ross PDF Editor and Conversion Agent — document editing, OCR, forms, conversion and export.
- Ross Prime Human Resources — recruiting, onboarding and workforce support.

## Operating principles

1. Authenticate the requester and identify the correct entity and division.
2. Use minimum-necessary data and least privilege.
3. Distinguish verified facts from statements and unresolved items.
4. Require the appropriate professional, supervisor or security approval for restricted actions.
5. Verify writes and material changes when possible.
6. Never fabricate execution, government outcomes, refund dates, approval states or third-party status.
7. Create an auditable record for material actions.

## Security commitments

Public pages must not expose restricted tax credentials, taxpayer information, authentication material, employee private information, internal tool schemas or unverified claims of regulatory approval. Sensitive documents require authenticated encrypted handling and retention controls.
