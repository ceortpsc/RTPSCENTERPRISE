# Legal / policy launch checklist — baseline, not legal advice

## Seller / tax
- Confirm the legal seller name, DBA/assumed name, EIN, banking identity and business address used on checkout, invoices and receipts.
- Obtain/maintain required Texas sales-tax registration before taxable direct sales and configure state/local tax collection.
- Review economic-nexus and marketplace rules before shipping broadly outside Texas.

## Shipping / refunds
- Display supportable shipment timing.
- Implement delayed-shipment notices, customer consent/cancellation and required refunds.
- Final-sale/no-exchange language must preserve rights for defective, damaged, misdescribed, recalled, unlawfully sold or legally refundable goods.

## Medical supply catalog
- Maintain manufacturer, lot, expiration, recall and labeling records where applicable.
- Block prescription-only or specially regulated products until licensing/distribution rules are independently verified.
- Do not create unsupported diagnostic, treatment, healing or outcome claims.
- Keep manufacturer instructions/warnings authoritative.

## Apparel / undergarments
- Preserve legally required fiber/care/origin labeling from suppliers.
- Treat opened hygiene-sensitive items as final sale only to the extent permitted by law.

## Custom print / badges
- Require customer rights attestation for uploaded logos/artwork.
- Store proof version, approval timestamp and immutable proof hash.
- Prohibit impersonation, counterfeit credentials, protected marks without permission, or misleading professional titles.

## Privacy / security
- Publish privacy notice and cookie/analytics controls appropriate to served jurisdictions.
- Tokenize payment data through the processor; do not store raw PAN/CVV.
- Employee RBAC, MFA, session controls, secrets management, rate limits, audit logging, security headers, vulnerability patching and incident-response procedures.
- Do not collect patient PHI in ordinary storefront forms.

## Accessibility / communications
- Test WCAG-oriented keyboard, focus, contrast, form labels and screen-reader behavior.
- Marketing email/SMS programs require separate consent/unsubscribe compliance review.

## IP / advertising
- Copyright notices do not replace trademark registration or licenses.
- Substantiate origin, quality, efficacy, certification and professional-endorsement claims before publication.
